Redlich–Kwong equation of state calculator (V4)

Calculate pressure/volume/temperature, compressibility factor, and fugacity coefficient for liquid/gas/supercritical fluid.

Usage:
  • Enter the critical temperature and pressure.
  • Enter two of three quantities, leaving blank the field to be calculated.
  • Select units.
  • Click  Calculate  to get the missing quantity, compressibility factor, and fugacity coefficient.
  • With T,P given, metastable and unstable phases are not calculated.
  • With Vm given, stability is reported for gas and liquid.
  • Simple expressions (e.g., 100+273.15) are accepted in input fields.
  • Spaces, tabs ignored in the input fields.
  • Both "." and "," can be used on input as the decimal separator.

Critical temperature = Temperature = °C K
Critical pressure = Pressure = Pa kPa bar atm   MPa
Molar volume = m³/mol dm³/mol cm³/mol

Results:
fluid:n.a.
gas:n.a.
liquid:n.a.

Notation:
  T = temperature
  P = pressure
  Vm = molar volume
  Tc = critical temperature
  Pc = critical pressure
  reduced quantities: Tr=T/Tc, Pr=P/Pc, Vr=Vm/(R*Tc/Pc)
  Z = Pr*Vr/Tr= P*Vm/(R*T) = compressibility factor
  φ = fugacity coefficient
  1 atm = 101 325 Pa, 1 bar = 100 000 Pa
